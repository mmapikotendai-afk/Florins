# Stage 2 homepage

Mode: Persuade, with garment-led editorial browsing. Code-led extension of the approved design system and the user's detailed Stage 2 composition brief.

THESIS: An appointment-only Harare fashion studio, expressed through original-colour garments, quiet monochrome typography and a cinematic film that never competes with text.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Solid white accessible masthead. An off-white editorial opening pairs the supplied headline and collection action with the unaltered 16:9 desktop film. On phones, copy precedes a full-width 9:16 film. Proportions and the whole frame take precedence over filling the viewport. No text overlays a model.

STORY: Film, introduction, staggered collection photographs, dark bespoke interlude, studio invitation, varied photographic gallery, monochrome consultation invitation and factual footer.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: Responsive source selection requests one film; reduced motion and data-saving preferences receive a still poster. Motion is confined to an already-visible heading reveal and gallery entrances. Navigation indicates the current section; mobile navigation restores focus and closes on Escape.

PROOF: Nine approved photographs, supplied logo and two prepared videos from the user-designated folder. No invented prices, product records, appointment slots or testimonials. The atelier photograph is captioned as a worn creation, not represented as the studio interior.

OPEN: Verified WhatsApp number and other business contacts; full collection and bespoke routes; booking rules and backend. Stage 2 links stay within the homepage or lead to the verified Instagram profile for enquiries. No backend or database work is authorized in this stage.

QUALITY BAR: Original garment colours and silhouettes remain intact, no crops through garments, distinct editorial pacing, complete navigation and readable type at 320–1920px. All requested sections remain present.
